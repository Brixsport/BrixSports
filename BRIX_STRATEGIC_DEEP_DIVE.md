# Brix: The Talent Hub Strategy (Strategic Deep-Dive)

## 1. The Vision: From "Viewing App" to "Talent Infrastructure"
Brix V2 is no longer just a way to watch games. It is a **Data Generation Engine**. Our goal is to own the most granular database of university and youth talent in the region. By providing free access to fans, we build the "Audience," and by logging professional stats, we create the "Inventory" that brands and scouts want to buy.

---

## 2. Revenue Stream A: The Sponsorship System
We monetize the *attention* of the fans. Instead of a paywall, we offer brands high-visibility real estate within the match experience.

### Inventory Hierarchy:
*   **The Scoreboard Sponsor**: A persistent, small logo next to the match timer (High-frequency visibility).
*   **Event-Driven Overlays**:
    *   **Goal Cards**: "GOAL! Powered by [Brand X]."
    *   **Half-Time Report**: "Halftime Summary brought to you by [Bank Y]."
    *   **Man of the Match**: A sponsored card at the end of the game with a QR code: "Claim your reward from [Brand Z]."
*   **Sponsored Chat Messages**: Automated system messages in the live chat every 15 minutes: "Stay energized with [Drink Brand]. Get a discount here: [Link]."

---

## 3. Revenue Stream B: The Scouting Hub (B2B Data)
This is our high-margin revenue path. Professional clubs and agencies spend millions searching for talent. Brix gives them the data they currently lack for African university sports.

### The "Data Product":
*   **Individual Player Heatmaps**: Generated by our loggers pinning events on a 2D Tactical Board.
*   **Expected Goals (xG)**: A calculated metric based on shot location and situation—available for every player.
*   **The Scout Feed**: A specialized API where agencies can filter for "High Skill" triggers (Eye Points). 
    *   *Example*: A scout searches for "Left-footed wingers with >10 Eye Points in the 18-yard box."

### B2B Tiers:
*   **Agency Tier**: Monthly fee for full access to the talent database and player export reports.
*   **Individual Scout Tier**: Pay-per-match access to granular data dossiers.

---

## 4. The Technical Edge: The "Tactical Board" Workflow
How do we get "Pro" data from a smartphone camera? We use **Human Intelligence** to solve the hardware problem.

### How it works (The "End-to-End" Solution):
1.  **Tiered Logging Priority**: 
    *   **Live (High-Value Only)**: The logger only "pins" locations for Goals, Shots, and major fouls (moments where the game naturally slows down).
    *   **Post-Match (Deep-Dive)**: All granular events (every pass, tackle, interception) are added after the match by watching the recording. This removes the stress of live "end-to-end" tracking.
2.  **The Interface**: On the logger's screen, the video player is in the center. On the right is a small, interactive 2D map of the pitch.
3.  **The Pinning Action**: The logger selects the event/player and then taps the exact spot on the **2D Map**. This takes less than 2 seconds.
4.  **The Result**: The system automatically merges the "Live" events and the "Post-Match" pins into one professional data dossier.
    *   *No 4K cameras needed.*
    *   *No AI training needed.*
    *   *Works on any internet connection.*

## 5. Why the Nigerian Market will adopt this
1.  **Zero Cost to Entry**: Students and fans hate paying. We give them the best sports app for free.
2.  **Brand Relevancy**: Large Nigerian brands (Telcos, Fintechs, Betting) are desperate to reach the Gen-Z university demographic. Brix is their direct "Live" bridge.
3.  **Path to Pro**: Players and parents will advocate for Brix because it creates an "Official Scout Record" that can get their kids signed to professional clubs.
