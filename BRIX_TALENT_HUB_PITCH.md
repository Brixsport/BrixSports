# Brix: The Talent Hub Strategy (Pitch)

## The Core Problem
Direct subscriptions are a barrier in many markets. Fans want free access, but the platform's data is too valuable to give away for nothing.

## The Pivot: From "B2C" to "B2B & Sponsorship"
We stop asking fans for money and start asking **Brands** and **Scouts** for value.

---

### 1. The "Free-to-Watch" Experience (Sponsorship)
We keep Brix 100% free for fans. Instead, we sell visibility.
- **Goal Overlays**: "This goal brought to you by [Brand Name]."
- **Live Assets**: Brand logos in the scoreboard and match highlights.
- **Affiliate Links**: "Buy the Jersey" or "Bet on the Match" links that generate commission.

### 2. The "Talent Hub" (B2B Scouting)
We turn our loggers into **Data Scouts**. Every match on Brix generates "Spatial Data" (Heatmaps, xG, Scout Tags).
- **The Scouting API**: Professional scouts and agencies pay for access to our talent database.
- **Individual Dossiers**: Every player gets a professional-grade profile with heatmaps and advanced stats, all captured from standard YouTube/Phone streams.
- **Scout Points**: Loggers flag "Elite highlights" that scouts can filter for instantly.

### 3. The "Low-Hardware" Edge
Our tech doesn't need 4K cameras. We use **Human Intelligence**.
- Loggers use a "Tactical 2D Board" to pin events while watching the stream.
- This creates **Elite-level data from Budget setups**, which is perfect for university sports.

---

## Why this wins
- **Fans**: Happy (free access).
- **Brands**: Happy (direct visibility to youth/university audience).
- **Scouts**: Happy (access to untapped African talent with deep data).
- **Brix**: Sustainable revenue through high-value data and brand partnerships.
